02 / INPUTS

Returns disagree. The market factor dominates.

Weekly returns from 2018–2026 are annualized to estimate μ and Σ. A crypto-market factor and a lagged four-week momentum factor explain much of the cross-asset variation.

05 / TAKEAWAYS

What the experiment actually teaches.

01

Instability is measurable—and reducible.

Every stabilization method reduces both gross exposure and sensitivity to small expected-return changes.

02

The most stable portfolio performs worst.

Cost-aware MVO has the lowest in-sample sensitivity but records a −0.67 out-of-sample Sharpe.

03

Equal weighting is a serious benchmark.

Its +0.68 Sharpe and 3.5% turnover outperform every μ-based method after costs.

04

The inputs are harder than the optimizer.

Matrix inversion is easy. Estimating returns reliably from a short, volatile crypto history is not.
